NETWORK MIDTERM TEST BANK
=========================

How to open
-----------
Double-click index.html. The app works locally and does not require an internet connection.

Contents
--------
- 150 total questions
- 110 multiple-choice questions
- 40 comparison questions with model answers
- 15 assignment-focused questions
- Source and PDF page citations for every question

Features
--------
- Show/hide answer independently for every question
- Search and filter by source, topic, question type, and priority
- Randomized practice quizzes with scoring and explanations
- Review incorrect answers
- Print questions with optional answers
- Light/dark themes

Priority labels
---------------
High: strongly emphasized, repeated, foundational, or assignment-linked.
Medium: directly testable supporting material.
Low: contextual detail or enrichment.

These labels are study priorities, not numeric predictions or guarantees. No test-bank generator can honestly guarantee that a question has more than a 51% chance of appearing without an official exam blueprint or past-exam evidence.
