How priorities are assigned

Priority labels organize study effort using evidence in the supplied course material. They do not express a numeric probability and do not guarantee that an item will appear on the exam.

High Strongly emphasized, repeated, or linked to an assignment or stated objective.
Medium Directly testable supporting material that helps explain a core concept.
Low Detail, context, or enrichment that may still support complete understanding.

Use citations to verify wording in the original material, and follow the professor’s announcements whenever they differ from this study aid.
